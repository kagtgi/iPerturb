The problem

Predicting a perturbation response

Kinetic models

  • Every parameter means something2,3
…but need measured rate constants — toy systems only.

Deep networks

  • CPA4 · GEARS5 · scGPT6 · Cell2Sentence7 · STATE8
…but almost none expose a readable signed mechanism.

Network propagation11

  • A proven amplifier — disease genes, GWAS, cancer drivers
…but assumes an undirected graph — the wrong shape for signed regulation.

We want propagation’s simplicity, without giving up direction, sign, or a readable mechanism.

Pillar 1 · step 3 of 3

Keep only the edges we can actually identify

1Harvest→2Merge→3Select

Three kinetic parameters per edge. Fit ~180,000 of them against 2,000 perturbations and nothing is identifiable.

So we cap capacity with a parameter budget20 and fill it in two passes:

  • Coverage first — walk the candidates by confidence so no gene is left without a regulator.
  • Then greedy fill — repeatedly take the best-supported remaining edge, but each pick penalises its own source and target, so no hub can swallow the budget.

Pillar 2 · learned kinetics

Regulation as a saturating switch21

\[ \class{tok tok-x}{x_i} \;=\; \frac{\class{tok tok-V}{V_i}\bigl(1+\class{tok tok-h}{h_i}\bigr) + \class{tok tok-b}{b_i}} {1+\class{tok tok-alpha}{\alpha_i}+\class{tok tok-r}{r_i}} \]
\[ \class{tok tok-h}{h_i}=\sum_{k\in\mathcal{N}_i} \class{tok tok-max}{\max}\Bigl(0,\; \frac{\class{tok tok-w}{w_{ki}}\,x_k^{\class{tok tok-n}{n_{ki}}}} {\class{tok tok-K}{K_{ki}}^{\class{tok tok-n}{n_{ki}}} +x_k^{\class{tok tok-n}{n_{ki}}}}\Bigr) \] \[ \class{tok tok-r}{r_i}=\sum_{k\in\mathcal{N}_i} \class{tok tok-max}{\max}\Bigl(0,\; \frac{-\class{tok tok-w}{w_{ki}}\,x_k^{\class{tok tok-n}{n_{ki}}}} {\class{tok tok-K}{K_{ki}}^{\class{tok tok-n}{n_{ki}}} +x_k^{\class{tok tok-n}{n_{ki}}}}\Bigr) \]
the equationOne gene's expression at equilibrium. \(V_i\) — maximal transcription rate How loud this gene can be shouted. \(b_i\) — basal expression A leaky baseline. The gene is never perfectly silent. \(\alpha_i\) — mRNA decay The cell is always clearing transcript. \(h_i\) — activators, in the numerator Activators push up — they multiply the ceiling. \(r_i\) — repressors, in the denominator Repressors push down — they divide it away. \(w_{ki}\) — regulatory weight Sign says activate or repress; magnitude says how strongly. Learned. \(K_{ki}\) — dissociation constant How much regulator you need for half an effect: the switch's threshold. \(n_{ki}\) — Hill coefficient How sharp the switch is — a graded dial, or an on/off toggle. the max(0, ·) pair One edge cannot both activate and repress. Its sign routes it to h or to r.
\(w\in(-1,1)\) via sigmoid / tanh  ·  \(n\in(1,4)\)  ·  \(K,V,b>0\) — every fitted parameter stays in a bounded, readable range.

Occupancy is exactly ½ when the regulator sits at its threshold — whatever K and n are.

Pillar 3 · why the update is biological

What one hop actually claims about a cell

  • Clamp, do not relax. CRISPRi holds the target down for the whole experiment, so the silenced gene is a boundary condition — not something the network is allowed to restore.
  • Every other gene recomputes from its regulators. Transcription is fast relative to the readout, so each gene sits at the level its current regulator occupancy dictates.
  • Iterate until nothing moves. The fixed point is the new steady state — which is exactly what a Perturb-seq profile measures, days after the knockdown.

And this is why it must be nonlinear

  • Saturation. Doubling a transcription factor does not double its target — the promoter fills up. The Hill term has a ceiling; a linear operator does not.
  • Signed composition. Activation through one path and repression through another must survive being combined.
GATA1 → BACH1 ⊣ NQO1 is precisely the loop from the motivation — and two slides ago you watched it resolve correctly.

Fitting

How we learn

\[ \begin{aligned} \mathcal{L}(\Theta) = {} & \class{tok tok-wmse}{\lambda_1\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{WMSE}}} + \class{tok tok-afda}{\lambda_2\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{AFDA}}} + \class{tok tok-bal}{\lambda_3\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{bal}}} \\[2pt] {} + {} & \class{tok tok-delta}{\lambda_4\mathcal{L}_{\Delta}} + \class{tok tok-deg}{\lambda_5\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{DEG}}} \end{aligned} \]
the objectiveFive terms, jointly minimised across every training perturbation. \(\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{WMSE}}\) — change-weighted reconstruction Get magnitude right, weighted by how much a gene actually moved. \(\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{AFDA}}\) — direction-aware focal loss Penalise wrong-sign predictions hardest — direction before magnitude. \(\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{bal}}\) — balance regulariser Guards against systematic up/down bias across the genome. \(\mathcal{L}_{\Delta}\) — change-vector reconstruction Fit the whole perturbation-response vector, not just the endpoint. \(\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{DEG}}\) — DEG-weighted Spend capacity on the genes that actually responded.

Adam, 50 epochs, cosine annealing.

(λ₁…λ₅) = (1, 1, 0.5, 1, 2)

Dropping any of the three reconstruction terms degrades both directional accuracy and MSE (backup). The two regularisers are kept for what they guard against, not for this table's numbers.

In summary

iPerturb

  • Real regulatory knowledge as the foundation — a curated, cell-line-specific GRN, not a learned graph.
  • Kinetics learned on it, then propagated multi-hop to a steady state.
  • So iPerturb predicts through a mechanism rather than explaining a prediction after the fact — and the paths it uses are testable hypotheses.

Discussion

Where this goes next

  • Mine mechanisms from the literature. Build a large database of regulatory mechanisms extracted from primary papers, and assemble the GRN from that rather than from aggregated interaction tables.
  • Add direct binding evidence. ChIP-seq and related assays, so an edge can be grounded in measured occupancy rather than inferred association.
  • Beyond CRISPRi. Genetic and chemical perturbations — a compound acting on a pathway is the same propagation problem with a different entry point.
  • Make it fast. Architectural work on the propagation solve, so the model scales to more cell lines and larger networks.

Appendix

Why it cannot answer a perturbation question

Linear propagation is not a heuristic. It is exactly the steady-state linear response of a kinetic model:

\[ \delta x^{*} = -J^{-1}B\,\delta u, \qquad -J^{-1}=(\gamma I-A)^{-1}=\tfrac{1}{\gamma}\sum_{k\ge 0}(A/\gamma)^{k} \]

— the random-walk-with-restart operator, recovered exactly.

That derivation is also its boundary: a first-order sensitivity, valid near the operating point and blind to saturation, cooperativity and signed feedback.
