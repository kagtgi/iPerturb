The problem

What we want to predict

\[ \class{tok tok-x0}{\mathbf{x}_0} \;\xrightarrow{\;\class{tok tok-p}{p}\;}\; \class{tok tok-xstar}{\mathbf{x}^*} \]
the taskOne perturbation in, one prediction out. \(\mathbf{x}_0\) — control 2,500 control cells per dataset, RNA-seq’d. The baseline everything is measured against. \(p\) — the perturbation CRISPRi holds one gene down for the whole experiment. \(\mathbf{x}^*\) — the perturbed state What Perturb-seq reads out, days later — and what we want to predict, without running the experiment.
1Motivation
The gap

Three existing answers, each with a gap

Kinetic models2,3

  • Every parameter means something
  • Explains the mechanism, not just the outcome
  • Needs measured rate constants
  • Only tractable on small systems

Deep networks

  • Scale to thousands of perturbations
  • Strong benchmark accuracy — CPA4 · GEARS5 · scGPT6 · Cell2Sentence7 · STATE8
  • The answer is a latent vector
  • Almost none expose a signed mechanism

Network propagation11

  • A proven amplifier — disease genes, GWAS, cancer drivers
  • Simple; nothing to train
  • Undirected, unsigned graph
  • Linear — cannot saturate or compose

We want a scalable, learnable method for data fitting, without giving up direction, sign, or a readable mechanism.

Pillar 2 · learned kinetics

Regulation as a saturating switch21

\[ \class{tok tok-x}{x_i} \;=\; \frac{\class{tok tok-V}{V_i}\bigl(1+\class{tok tok-h}{h_i}\bigr) + \class{tok tok-b}{b_i}} {1+\class{tok tok-alpha}{\alpha_i}+\class{tok tok-r}{r_i}} \]
\[ \class{tok tok-h}{h_i}=\sum_{k\in\class{tok tok-N}{\mathcal{N}_i}} \class{tok tok-max}{\max}\Bigl(0,\; \frac{\class{tok tok-w}{w_{ki}}\,\class{tok tok-x}{x_k}^{\class{tok tok-n}{n_{ki}}}} {\class{tok tok-K}{K_{ki}}^{\class{tok tok-n}{n_{ki}}} +\class{tok tok-x}{x_k}^{\class{tok tok-n}{n_{ki}}}}\Bigr) \] \[ \class{tok tok-r}{r_i}=\sum_{k\in\class{tok tok-N}{\mathcal{N}_i}} \class{tok tok-max}{\max}\Bigl(0,\; \frac{-\class{tok tok-w}{w_{ki}}\,\class{tok tok-x}{x_k}^{\class{tok tok-n}{n_{ki}}}} {\class{tok tok-K}{K_{ki}}^{\class{tok tok-n}{n_{ki}}} +\class{tok tok-x}{x_k}^{\class{tok tok-n}{n_{ki}}}}\Bigr) \]
the equationOne gene’s expression at equilibrium — everything in brick is fitted from data. \(V_i\) — maximal transcription rate How loud this gene can be shouted. \(b_i\) — basal expression A leaky baseline. The gene is never perfectly silent. \(\alpha_i\) — mRNA decay The cell is always clearing transcript. \(h_i\) — activators, in the numerator Activators push up — they multiply the ceiling. \(r_i\) — repressors, in the denominator Repressors push down — they divide it away. \(w_{ki}\) — regulatory weight Sign says activate or repress; magnitude says how strongly. Learned. \(K_{ki}\) — dissociation constant How much regulator you need for half an effect: the switch's threshold. \(n_{ki}\) — Hill coefficient How sharp the switch is — a graded dial, or an on/off toggle. the max(0, ·) pair One edge cannot both activate and repress. Its sign routes it to h or to r.
learned \(V, b, \alpha, w, K, n\)  ·  computed each hop \(x, h, r\)  ·  fixed by the GRN \(\mathcal{N}_i\)
\(w\in(-1,1)\) via sigmoid / tanh  ·  \(n\in(1,4)\)  ·  \(K,V,b>0\) — every fitted parameter stays in a bounded, readable range.

Occupancy is exactly ½ when the regulator sits at its threshold — whatever K and n are.

Pillar 3 · why the update is biological

What one hop actually claims about a cell

  • Clamp, don’t relax. CRISPRi holds the gene down — a boundary condition, not something the network restores.
  • Every other gene recomputes. Each sits where its regulators put it.
  • Iterate to a fixed point. That is what Perturb-seq measures, days later.

And this is why it must be nonlinear

  • Saturation. Promoters fill up. Hill has a ceiling; a linear operator does not.
  • Signed composition. Activation and repression must survive being combined.
GATA1 → BACH1 ⊣ NQO1 — the loop from the motivation, resolved.
2Methods
Iteration stops at \(\|x^{(t+1)}-x^{(t)}\|_2 < 10^{-5}\), capped at 100 hops. Median 13 hops, no non-convergent case on either cell line.
Fitting

How we learn

\[ \mathcal{L}(\Theta) = \class{tok tok-wmse}{\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{WMSE}}} + \class{tok tok-afda}{\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{AFDA}}} + \class{tok tok-bal}{\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{bal}}} + \class{tok tok-delta}{\mathcal{L}_{\Delta}} + \class{tok tok-deg}{\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{DEG}}} \]
the objectiveFive terms, jointly minimised across every training perturbation. \(\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{WMSE}}\) — change-weighted reconstruction Get magnitude right, weighted by how much a gene actually moved. \(\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{AFDA}}\) — direction-aware focal loss Penalise wrong-sign predictions hardest — direction before magnitude. \(\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{bal}}\) — balance regulariser Guards against systematic up/down bias across the genome. \(\mathcal{L}_{\Delta}\) — change-vector reconstruction Fit the whole perturbation-response vector, not just the endpoint. \(\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{DEG}}\) — DEG-weighted Spend capacity on the genes that actually responded.

In summary

iPerturb

  • Real regulatory knowledge as the foundation — a curated, cell-line-specific GRN, not a learned graph.
  • Kinetics learned on it, then propagated multi-hop to a steady state.
  • So iPerturb predicts through a mechanism rather than explaining a prediction after the fact — and the paths it uses are testable hypotheses.

4Conclusion
Discussion

Where this goes next

  • Mine mechanisms from the literature. Build a large database of regulatory mechanisms extracted from primary papers, and assemble the GRN from that rather than from aggregated interaction tables.
  • Add direct binding evidence. ChIP-seq and related assays, so an edge can be grounded in measured occupancy rather than inferred association.
  • Beyond CRISPRi. Genetic and chemical perturbations — a compound acting on a pathway is the same propagation problem with a different entry point.
  • Make it fast. Architectural work on the propagation solve, so the model scales to more cell lines and larger networks.

Appendix

Why it cannot answer a perturbation question

Linear propagation is not a heuristic. It is exactly the steady-state linear response of a kinetic model:

\[ \delta x^{*} = -J^{-1}B\,\delta u, \qquad -J^{-1}=(\gamma I-A)^{-1}=\tfrac{1}{\gamma}\sum_{k\ge 0}(A/\gamma)^{k} \]

— the random-walk-with-restart operator, recovered exactly.

That derivation is also its boundary: a first-order sensitivity, valid near the operating point and blind to saturation, cooperativity and signed feedback.
