Network propagation, from a toy regulatory network to a real association network. (a) Signed directed toy network of seven nodes (green arrows activate, red bars repress; all edge confidences 0.9) with one CRISPRi-perturbed gene P held (clamped, lock) at a reduced level (Δx = −1) at every step. Panels show the predicted |Δx| of the six other nodes after t = 0, 1, 2, 3 hops (cumulated hop contributions) and at the steady state (t = ∞) of the clamped model of ipt/explain/mechanism.py, with the weight matrix rescaled to spectral radius ρ = 0.9 (raw radius 1.10); node colour is |Δx| on a white-to-blue scale saturating at 0.5, and P is drawn in solid blue. (b) The same toy graph with unsigned undirected edges: random walk with restart from P (restart probability α = 0.3, p_k = α p_0 + (1 − α) W p_(k−1), W = A D^(−1)) for iterations 0–9; rows are nodes, columns iterations, colour is the probability p. The largest per-node change between consecutive iterations first falls below 1e-3 at k = 14. (c) K562 association network (STRING plus curated pairs, undirected, unsigned; 8563 genes). Seeds are the fold-0 test targets KIF4A (blue) and TTK (yellow), chosen as the pair with different three-letter gene-name prefixes, each with at least 15 neighbours, and the largest Jaccard overlap of two-hop neighbourhoods (0.93). The subnetwork is the two seeds plus the 45 genes with the highest maximum random-walk-with-restart score (α = 0.5). Left: genes that are direct (one-hop) neighbours of KIF4A (blue), of TTK (yellow) or of both (green); all other genes white. Right: the same genes coloured by the larger of the two seeds' propagated scores (random walk with restart, α = 0.5, W = A D^(−1)) on a logarithmic scale. Direct neighbours: 86 for KIF4A, 96 for TTK. Over all 8563 genes, 86 of the 172 highest-scoring genes of KIF4A (seed excluded) are not direct neighbours (reached at hop 2 or more), and 96 of the 192 highest-scoring genes of TTK. Share of the propagated mass on non-seed genes at hop 1, hop 2 and hop 3 or more: KIF4A 74.7%, 21.8%, 3.5%; TTK 74.0%, 22.5%, 3.5%. The seed itself holds 50.3% (KIF4A) and 50.2% (TTK) of the total mass. Node positions are a layout, not geometry: horizontal position is the rank of log(score from KIF4A / score from TTK) and vertical position the rank of the smaller of the two scores; only edges incident to a seed are drawn. Seeds are outlined; labelled are the seeds and the five non-seed nodes with the highest minimum of the two scores.
