**Fig. 1. iPerturb predicts a context-specific single-cell perturbation response by propagating a CRISPRi knockdown through an evidence-weighted gene regulatory network (GRN) and explains it with the same network.** (1) Inputs: a cell line (K562 or RPE1) with a CRISPRi target gene, and Perturb-seq control cells with the training perturbations; the learning target is the mean expression response. (2) Context-specific GRN: five evidence tiers are merged into one network per cell line (K562 and RPE1 graphs differ). L1 curated signed TF-target regulation (CollecTRI, OmniPath, TRRUST) is the only tier drawn as signed arrows (green, activation; red bar, repression). L2 GeneHancer enhancer and TFBS evidence active in the cell line, L3 STRING protein association (score >= 700) and L4 COXPRESdb coexpression are association evidence and are drawn as undirected, unsigned dashed grey lines; L5 is derived from the training-perturbation responses (small response strip). (3) Learnable Hill-form propagation: a Hill-shaped transfer curve H(x;k) is evaluated through a Chebyshev basis with sparse or dense matrix multiplication, the perturbed gene is persistently clamped (lock), and the iteration is run to a fixed point x*. (4) Output: predicted expression change per gene (bars: up, yellow; down, purple; schematic, not data). (5) Explanation: the top affected genes with their supporting neighbours and paths, validated by edge and neighbour ablation. Black arrows show data flow. This is a schematic; no measured values are plotted, and the K562/RPE1 graphs and bar heights are illustrative.
