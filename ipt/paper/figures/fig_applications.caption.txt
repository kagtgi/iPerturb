Applications of network propagation in iPerturb on the K562 essential-gene screen (fold-0 split; association network = STRING plus curated pairs, undirected, 8563 genes). (a) Left: random-walk-with-restart (α = 0.5) neighbourhood of the held-out ribosomal-protein seed RPL11: the seed and its 30 highest-scoring genes; node area of the 30 neighbours is proportional to their propagation score (the seed, which holds most of the mass, is drawn at a fixed size, red outline; only edges from the seed are drawn and nodes sit on a ring, so position carries no information; the eight highest-scoring genes are labelled); 30 of the 30 genes are ribosomal proteins (RPL/RPS, blue), and none are grey. Right: observed perturbation responses (Δ) of the 20 genes with the largest mean |Δ| over the 61 training RPL/RPS perturbations (rows), for 8 of them (columns, randomly chosen, fixed seed); the mean pairwise Pearson correlation of the Δ profiles of all 61 training ribosomal perturbations is 0.52. (b) Each of the 269 fold-0 test perturbations with a propagated prediction is a node; predictions come from propagating training responses over the network (α = 0.5, own response excluded), centred on the mean over nodes; each node is joined to its 3 nearest neighbours by cosine similarity (608 distinct edges). Colours: gene family from the symbol prefix. 86.0% of the 43 edges between two nodes that both have a family join nodes of the same family, against 21.1% expected by chance (sum of squared family frequencies among the 31 nodes with a family); counting all 608 edges, 6.1% are same-family. Layout: spring layout, fixed seed. (c) Left: schematic of the integration scheme. Right: fitted non-negative-least-squares weights of the integrated model per tier graph, mean over the folds present (K562: 3 folds (0, 1, 2); RPE1: 3 folds (0, 1, 2)). Weights above 0.005: K562: L3 STRING 0.44, L4 COXPRES 0.08, SIGNOR 0.05, CORUM 0.37, BioGRID 0.05, GTEx co-expr. 0.01; RPE1: L3 STRING 0.44, L4 COXPRES 0.11, SIGNOR 0.01, CORUM 0.44.
