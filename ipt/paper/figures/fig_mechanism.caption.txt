Fig. X. Mechanism of the clamped steady-state propagation, and how far its signs can be trusted. (a) Toy network computed with the iPerturb functions build_W (spectral radius 0.8) and clamped_response: A activates B, B represses C, B activates D, D represses B. CRISPRi clamps A (lock) at dx = -1; the predicted responses are B -0.46, C +0.30 and D -0.30 (blue = down, red = up; fill scaled to the largest magnitude), i.e. B down, C up, D down. (b) Sign accuracy of the steady-state prediction among responding genes (|z| > 3) on held-out targets, by shortest-path distance from the perturbed gene, for the real signed network (blue), permuted edge signs (grey) and degree-preserving rewired edges (yellow). Hits and counts are pooled over the five draws of each control kind; error bars are Wilson 95% intervals; the dashed line marks 0.5; the number at hop 1 is the count of responding genes for the real network (K562 114, RPE1 358). The controls lie above 0.5 because most responding genes move in the same direction, so a prediction dominated by one sign scores above chance; the controls, not 0.5, are the relevant reference. At hop 1 the real signs reach 0.83 (K562) and 0.76 (RPE1) against 0.73 and 0.70 for permuted signs and 0.69 and 0.58 for rewired edges. At hops 2 to 4 the real signs exceed the permuted-sign control by no more than 0.01 in any cell of either line. (c) Mechanism report for SSRP1 (K562, fold 0): the five reached genes with the largest |z|, and the strongest signed path to each (all run through MYC at hop 2; all edges are activations); the supporting databases of each edge are listed under the diagram. Node fill shows the observed z, the triangle the predicted sign (all five predicted down), and the ring marks whether the sign is correct (green) or wrong (red): RPL9, RPL21, RPL5 correct; RPLP1, UBA52 wrong. (d) Real-network sign accuracy at hop 1 and at hops 2 and above with Wilson 95% intervals (K562 0.83, n = 114, and 0.53, n = 5237; RPE1 0.76, n = 358, and 0.53, n = 15734). Right: Pearson correlation between predicted and observed change over reached genes for each held-out target (dots) with the mean as a bar (K562 mean 0.016 over 33 targets; RPE1 mean 0.012 over 47 targets). Only signed curated edges are used; association edges are excluded.
