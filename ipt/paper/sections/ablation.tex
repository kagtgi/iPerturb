\subsection{Evidence tiers and multi-hop propagation}
\label{sec:ablation_tiers}
\label{sec:ablation_layers}
To measure what each tier contributes, we propagated the observed training responses over each tier alone (Table~\ref{tab:tiers}), in the manner of network propagation~\cite{cowen2017network}.
Protein-association evidence carries the predictive signal: STRING alone reaches $\rho_c=$ \datanum{g12_k562_prop_L3__rwr} (K562) and \datanum{g12_rpe1_prop_L3__rwr} (RPE1), and CORUM complexes and BioGRID interactions are informative on their own, whereas the curated regulatory tier alone does not improve on the linear baseline (\datanum{g12_k562_B2_linear} and \datanum{g12_rpe1_B2_linear}) and the co-expression tiers improve on it only modestly; adding signed SIGNOR edges to the curated tier changes $\rho_c$ by \datanum{g12d_k562_signor_l1} (K562) and \datanum{g12d_rpe1_signor_l1} (RPE1).
Reading the curated-plus-STRING graph beyond the first hop improves on the one-hop neighbour mean, by \datanum{g12d_k562_rwr_one} (K562) and \datanum{g12d_rpe1_rwr_one} (RPE1) for random walks with restart and by \datanum{g12d_k562_trunc_one} and \datanum{g12d_rpe1_trunc_one} for walks truncated after two or three steps.
Propagating each tier separately and integrating the read-outs with non-negative weights adds a further \datanum{g12d_k562_int_rwr} (K562) and \datanum{g12d_rpe1_int_rwr} (RPE1).
The fitted weights concentrate on protein association (K562: STRING \datanum{g12w_k562_L3}, CORUM \datanum{g12w_k562_corum}, BioGRID \datanum{g12w_k562_biogrid}, COXPRESdb \datanum{g12w_k562_L4}; RPE1: STRING \datanum{g12w_rpe1_L3}, CORUM \datanum{g12w_rpe1_corum}; Figure~\ref{fig:applications}c), so the curated tier contributes explanation rather than prediction.
Figure~\ref{fig:ablation}b summarises the matched controls: only the real association evidence changes $\rho_c$ relative to no graph, while rewired and data-derived graphs with the same read-out do not.

\subsection{Cell-line-specific tier}
\label{sec:ablation_context}
\datanumif{g7_d_k_hep}{We tested in K562 whether the cell-line tier carries signal specific to its own line, with a criterion registered before the run: the K562 GeneHancer tier must beat the HepG2 and Jurkat tiers when added to the curated-plus-STRING graph.
Adding the K562 tier changes $\rho_c$ by \datanum{g7_d_k_base} relative to the base graph, by \datanum{g7_d_k_hep} relative to the HepG2 tier and by \datanum{g7_d_k_jur} relative to the Jurkat tier, so the criterion was not met; the enlarged graph remains better than its rewired version by \datanum{g7_d_k_rew}.
The cell-line tier thus shapes the network and its explanations (Section~\ref{sec:results_grn}), while the predictive signal is carried by association evidence that is shared between lines; the ChIP-Atlas tier behaves alike (Table~\ref{tab:tiers}).}

\subsection{Hill versus linear transfer}
\label{sec:ablation_hill}
We trained the regulator-shared Hill model and a linear model with the same graph, decoder and budget as a residual on the linear baseline, on the targets reachable from the signed core (\datanum{k562_g2_reach_n} K562 and \datanum{rpe1_g2_reach_n} RPE1 unique targets; Figure~\ref{fig:ablation}a).
The two transfer functions predict equally well: the Hill form differs from the linear form by \datanum{k562_g2_h2_b3} (K562) and \datanum{rpe1_g2_h2_b3} (RPE1), and from the no-graph baseline by \datanum{k562_g2_h2_b2} and \datanum{rpe1_g2_h2_b2}.
The same holds for a certified variant with a log-odds state, a leaky Hill transfer and a gain that is a contraction for every parameter value (Appendix~\ref{app:convergence}), trained with a mean-squared or a correlation loss (\datanum{g10d_k562_gn2_hill4_mse} and \datanum{g10d_k562_gn2_hill4_corr} in K562, \datanum{g10d_rpe1_gn2_hill4_mse} and \datanum{g10d_rpe1_gn2_hill4_corr} in RPE1, relative to no graph; Table~\ref{tab:certhill}).
An oracle read-out with its scale fitted on the test targets adds nothing, so the limit is the reach of the signed core (\datanum{k562_signed_out_pct}--\datanum{rpe1_signed_out_pct}\% of perturbed genes have a signed out-edge) rather than the transfer function or the optimiser.
The Hill operator therefore serves as the faithful, convergent and efficient regulatory model of iPerturb and as its explanation channel, while prediction is carried by the association tiers.

\subsection{Depth of signed paths}
\label{sec:ablation_hops}
The sign accuracy of the clamped steady state decreases with regulatory distance: at two hops it is \datanum{mech_k562_real_h2} (K562) and \datanum{mech_rpe1_real_h2} (RPE1), at three hops \datanum{mech_k562_real_h3} and \datanum{mech_rpe1_real_h3}, and at four or more hops \datanum{mech_k562_real_h4p} and \datanum{mech_rpe1_real_h4p}, at the level of the permuted-sign control (Figure~\ref{fig:mechanism}b,d).
Responses far from the perturbed gene are dominated by shared stress and growth programmes, which the association tiers capture; signed paths beyond the first hop are therefore reported as candidate mechanisms.

\label{sec:ablation_prep}
